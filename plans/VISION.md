# Bottom Up — Vision

> **Helping students build their careers from the bottom up.**

Bottom Up is a three-sided career platform connecting **students, employers, and schools** in Singapore.

Today, early-career hiring is fragmented. Students search across school portals, job platforms, and company websites. Employers manage multiple recruitment channels and manual processes. Career centres spend significant time coordinating between students and employers. The result is unnecessary friction, missed opportunities, and inefficient hiring.

Bottom Up brings these three sides into one **career ecosystem**.

For students, Bottom Up makes it easier to discover relevant internships and jobs, receive timely opportunities, and apply with less effort.

For employers, it makes it easier to reach relevant early-career talent and reduce the administrative burden of hiring.

For schools, it streamlines career-centre workflows and strengthens their ability to connect students with employers and improve employment outcomes.

The name **Bottom Up** reflects the way careers are built. Students typically start with their first internship or work experience, build skills and connections, and progress toward full-time employment. Bottom Up exists to support this journey from its earliest steps onward.

The initial focus is simple:

> **Make early-career hiring more efficient and outcome-driven for everyone involved.**

We will start by solving the fundamental problems of **discovery, application, and hiring**. As the platform matures, it can expand into deeper school workflows, employer collaboration, career insights, and other capabilities that strengthen the ecosystem.

The long-term vision is to build a **career ecosystem where students, employers, and schools no longer have to navigate disconnected platforms and processes.**

---

## Principles

### Outcome over activity

We measure success by better outcomes — relevant opportunities found, internships secured, roles filled, and students supported — rather than engagement for its own sake.

### Reduce friction

Every unnecessary search, account, email, form, and manual process is an opportunity to make the experience better.

### Signal over noise

Better opportunities are more valuable than more opportunities. Relevance, freshness, and trust should guide discovery.

### Three sides, one ecosystem

Students, employers, and schools are interconnected. The platform should create value across all three sides rather than optimise one at the expense of another.

### Build the foundation first

The platform comes first. Future intelligence and automation should emerge from real user needs and proven workflows, rather than defining the product prematurely.

---

## Product Evolution

### Phase 1 — Discover

Help students find relevant internships and jobs without searching across fragmented platforms.

### Phase 2 — Apply

Make applications faster and less repetitive.

### Phase 3 — Hire

Help employers screen, coordinate, and hire more efficiently.

### Phase 4 — Connect

Bring schools, students, and employers into a more integrated career ecosystem.

### Future — Assist

Explore intelligent and agentic workflows where they can meaningfully reduce effort or improve outcomes.

The sequence is intentional:

> **First solve the workflow. Then improve it. Then automate it.**

---

## North Star

Bottom Up starts with the first step of a student's career.

An internship becomes experience.
Experience becomes opportunity.
Opportunity becomes employment.
Employment becomes a career.

**Bottom Up helps students build that journey — from the bottom up.**
